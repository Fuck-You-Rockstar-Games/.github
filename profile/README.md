# Fuck-You-Rockstar-Games
> All texts are translated into English using AI!

I used to love Rockstar Games and their titles. I bought every single game for both console and PC! I’ve loved the multiplayer community ever since GTA San Andreas and SA-MP—a scene built by the community, for the community (FOR FREE)!

Unfortunately, Rockstar Games changed over time. The days when we, the players, were their top priority are long gone. Ever since their acquisition of the multiplayer mod 'FiveM', it’s no longer about us. Modifications that brought us together as a community and where we made genuine friends—like 'RageMP' and 'alt:V'—were forced to shut down just to herd everyone over to their side and maximize profits through their subscription model.

I’m offering you projects here that can be used for 'private purposes' **\*wink\***. No platform is being provided to list servers or similar! These projects are strictly for educational purposes and to offer a little insight into how these kinds of implementations work **\*wink\***.
